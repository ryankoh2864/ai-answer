    Questions

    Good questions to ask first.

    Is the first call really free?

    Yes. It's 30 minutes on your goal, and you get a written proposal afterwards. There's nothing to pay unless you say yes to it.

    Do I need to be technical?

    No. If you can use email and join a video call, you can do this. I do the technical parts and write everything down in plain English.

    Which AI tools will you recommend?

    Whatever fits your goal. That might be ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a smaller tool, or something that runs on your own computer. I test them on your actual task before I recommend anything.

    What does "private AI on your own computer" mean?

    An AI assistant that runs on a machine you own, instead of only inside someone else's app. I set it up with Hermes Agent, which is free and open source. You choose which AI model it uses, including models that run entirely on your own computer, so your files can stay with you.

    It doesn't send usage data anywhere, and your chats and its memory are stored on your own machine. A model that runs fully offline needs a fairly strong computer, so I check yours before you buy anything. The install itself is the easy part. What you're paying for is a setup that's locked down properly and doing real jobs for you.

    What do I pay on top of your fee?

    Only the AI tools you decide to keep, paid directly to the company that makes them, in your name. The main AI plans cost about US$20 a month. For a private AI, you may also want a computer to run it on, and I'll tell you before you buy anything.

    What if my goal is too big for one week?

    Then your proposal will say so, with a realistic date. Usually we pick the part that gives you the most time back and get that working first.

    Which time zones do you work with?

    I'm in Kuala Lumpur (GMT+8). Daytime calls suit Asia and Australia, late afternoons suit Europe and the Middle East, and early mornings or late evenings suit the Americas.

    Can I get a refund?
